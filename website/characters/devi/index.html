Gods & Puranas

Devi

देवी

Durga · Mahishasuramardini

The Devi Mahatmya calls her eternal, the world itself her form, said to be born whenever she appears for the gods’ sake. When Mahishasura seizes heaven, the light of every god gathers into her; armed by them and riding a lion, she slays him, and the gods praise her as Durga. To King Suratha and the merchant Samadhi, who worship her clay image for three years, she grants what each asks: a kingdom, and knowledge.

Study dossierArchetype, turning point and teaching, for those who want to go deeper

Archetype & symbolism

Power that answers when it is asked. In the Devi Mahatmya the goddess rises from the combined light of the gods and meets a king and a merchant who have lost everything with the same question: what do you want?

The defining moral crucible

Suratha asked for his kingdom back; Samadhi asked for knowledge. The goddess granted both without ranking them.

Core teaching

Two people can ask differently and both be answered.

दो लोग अलग-अलग माँग सकते हैं और दोनों को उत्तर मिल सकता है।
