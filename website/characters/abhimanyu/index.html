Mahabharata

Abhimanyu

अभिमन्यु

Saubhadra · Son of Arjuna

Abhimanyu, son of Arjuna and Krishna’s sister Subhadra, is the boy who broke into the wheel array alone; the epic says he was the Moon’s son Varchas, lent to earth for sixteen years. He knew the way in, not the way out, and went in when Yudhishthira asked. He scattered the Kauravas till six great warriors fell on him; bowless, he raised a chariot wheel like Krishna lifting the discus. His son Parikshit carried on the Kuru line.

Where this comes from

The notes this page about Abhimanyu was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. Arjuna weds Subhadra, the younger sister of Vasudeva (Krishna). Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Adi Parva, Section LXI
  2. Soma lets his son Varchas be born as Arjuna's son, to stay on earth sixteen years and fight alone in the Chakra-vyuha. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Adi Parva, Section LXVII
  3. Abhimanyu: his father taught him how to penetrate this array, but "I shall not be able ... to come out"; Yudhishthira asks him to break it. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Drona Parva, Section XXXIII
  4. Karna cuts off his bow, Kritavarman slays his steeds and Kripa his charioteers; bowless and carless he takes up sword and shield and leaps into the sky. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Drona Parva, Section XLVI
  5. He takes up a car-wheel, then a mace; he is slain "while fighting singly, by six mighty car-warriors of the Dhartarashtra army, headed by Drona and Karna". Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Drona Parva, Section XLVII
  6. Parikshit, "the mighty son of Abhimanyu", is born of Uttara when the Kuru race was almost extinct. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Adi Parva, Section XLIX
  7. He is called "the mighty car-warrior Saubhadra" (son of Subhadra). Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Bhishma Parva, Section XLIV
  8. Holding a car-wheel in his upraised arms, Abhimanyu, "imitating Vasudeva (with his discus)", rushes at Drona. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Drona Parva, Section XLVI
