Duryodhana

दुर्योधन

Suyodhana · Eldest of the Kauravas

Eldest son of Dhritarashtra and Gandhari, a pupil of Balarama at the mace; when Karna's lineage was questioned, he crowned him king of Anga on the spot. But the epic calls him a portion of Kali: envy of the Pandavas led him to the dice, to Draupadi's humiliation in the hall, and to war. Bhima's mace broke his thighs as vowed; he lay proud to the last, and flowers rained on him from the sky.
