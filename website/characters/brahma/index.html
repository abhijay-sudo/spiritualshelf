Brahma

ब्रह्मा

Pitamaha · Srishtikarta

Brahma, Pitamaha, grandfather of all that lives, is revered as creator of the worlds; through him every tapas bears its fruit. He bade Valmiki sing all of Rama’s story, to be told as long as mountains and rivers last, and after the war told Rama: you are Narayana, and Sita is Lakshmi.

Read more

He drove Shiva’s chariot against the three cities. Once, the Shiva Purana says, he claimed in pride to have seen the top of Shiva’s pillar of fire; spared at Vishnu’s plea, he was made presiding god of every sacrifice.

Where this comes from

What this page about Brahma was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. Name and title: grandfather of all that lives (paraphrase; Yuddha Khanda 1.26; Sanskrit lokapitamaha, 1.54) and “creator of the worlds” (2.7, 6.39; Sanskrit srishtikarta, 1.54). Sources of the story “Shiva and Tripura”
  2. For the lines “gives the fruit of every tapas” and “creator of everything”: Brahma’s own words to Taraka’s sons, Yuddha Khanda 1.28. Sources of the story “Shiva and Tripura”
  3. “whatever is born must die”: Yuddha Khanda 1.36-41. Sources of the story “Shiva and Tripura”
  4. Grants the three cities and orders Maya to build them: Yuddha Khanda 1.54-58. Sources of the story “Shiva and Tripura”
  5. Will not destroy what grew by his favour: Yuddha Khanda 2.8-10. Sources of the story “Shiva and Tripura”
  6. Charioteer of the chariot made of the worlds: Yuddha Khanda 8.24 and 9.1, 9.11. Sources of the story “Shiva and Tripura”
  7. Prays to be Shiva’s charioteer always: Yuddha Khanda 11.22-24. Sources of the story “Shiva and Tripura”
  8. As a swan he flies up to find the top of the column; a falling ketaki flower tells him it cannot be reached; he has it bear false witness that he saw the top. Shiva Purana, Vidyeshvara Samhita 7.16, 7.19–28
  9. Pride: Shiva says he played the lord by deceit to win honour; Shiva rose in the battle to quell their pride. Shiva Purana, Vidyeshvara Samhita 8.9; 9.33
  10. Bhairava seizes his fifth head; Vishnu weeps and pleads; Shiva calls Bhairava off; Brahma is to have no temple or festival, but is made the presiding deity of every sacrifice, without whom none bears fruit. Shiva Purana, Vidyeshvara Samhita 8.1–14
  11. Bids Valmiki compose the whole story of Rama in the shloka metre and says it will be told as long as mountains and rivers last (the same record as Valmiki’s) Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 2, verses 30–37
  12. Tells Rama after the war: you are Narayana; Sita is Lakshmi; you entered a human body to slay Ravana (the same record as Rama’s) Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda, Sarga 117, verses 13–28
About these sources
  • The Tripura parts of this account are drawn only from the Rudra Samhita, Yuddha Khanda, chapters 1–12 (wisdomlib.org, Shastri translation, paraphrased; Sanskrit checked on sa.wikisource.org).
