Shukracharya

शुक्राचार्य

Daityaguru · Kavi Ushanas

Shukracharya, also Ushanas and Kavya, is the seer of Bhrigu’s line and guru of the asuras, whom the Bhagavata calls best of the learned; in the Gita Krishna says, “Among seers I am Ushanas.” His Sanjivani brought the fallen Bali back to life.

He knew Vamana for Vishnu at once and warned Bali not to give, and when the king would not break his word, cursed him to lose his fortune. Later, at Vishnu’s request, he completed Bali’s sacrifice.

Names

Shukracharya. Also known as: Daityaguru, Kavi Ushanas.

Where this comes from

What this page about Shukracharya was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. ‘Daityaguru’ is his traditional designation as preceptor of the asuras; the Bhagavata calls him their family preceptor and guru. Traditional epithet; Bhagavata Purana 8.20.1 (kulācāryeṇa), 8.20.14 (guruḥ)
  2. For his title and line: Krishna says, among seers (kavis) I am Ushanas. Bhagavad Gita 10.37 (kavīnām uśanā kaviḥ)
  3. Called Ushanas, ‘best of the knowing’ (vidāṁ varaḥ); recognises Vamana as Vishnu and warns Bali: one step the earth, the second heaven, no place for the third. Bhagavata Purana 8.19.29–34
  4. Called Kavya. Bhagavata Purana 7.10.33 (kāvyādibhiḥ)
  5. Revives the fallen asuras and Bali with his Sanjivani knowledge. Bhagavata Purana 8.11.47–48
  6. Of the line of Bhrigu; Bali, brought back to life by the Bhrigus, becomes his disciple. Bhagavata Purana 8.15.3–6, 8.15.28
  7. Curses Bali to lose his fortune when he will not break his promise. Bhagavata Purana 8.20.14–15
  8. At the Lord’s request completes Bali’s sacrifice. Bhagavata Purana 8.23.13–18
