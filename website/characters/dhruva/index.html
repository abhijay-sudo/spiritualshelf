Dhruva

ध्रुव

Bhakta Dhruva

Son of King Uttanapada and Queen Suniti, Dhruva is remembered as the child whose resolve nothing could bend. Wounded by his stepmother’s words, he took the mantra Narada gave him to Madhuvana, and within six months his austerity brought Vishnu before him. He was granted the pole star, around which the stars circle; he ruled the earth for thirty-six thousand years, then rose to that place.

Study dossierArchetype, turning point and teaching, for those who want to go deeper

Archetype & symbolism

The child who turned rejection into resolve. Dhruva stands for : the slow inner heat that makes a small person fixed, like the pole star named after him.

The defining moral crucible

Denied his father’s lap and stung by his stepmother’s words, he could have hated. He went to the forest and sought something that could not be taken away.

Core teaching

A fixed point is made, not found.

अटल बिंदु मिलता नहीं, बनाया जाता है।
