Ramayana

Bharata

भरत

Bhratri-vatsala

Bharata, Kaikeyi’s son, is the brother Valmiki calls bhratri-vatsala, devoted to his brothers. Home from Kekaya to find the throne claimed for him, he cried: what use is a kingdom to me? He followed Rama to Chitrakuta, and when Rama would not break their father’s word, carried home his sandals and enthroned them. Fourteen years at Nandigrama he lived in bark and matted hair, laid every matter before them, then gave back the kingdom he had held in trust.

Where this comes from

The notes this page about Bharata was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. On hearing of his father’s death and Rama’s exile: “what use is a kingdom to me?”; he reproaches his mother Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 73, verses 1–12
  2. At Chitrakuta begs Rama to return; receives the sandals; vows fourteen years as an ascetic and to enter the fire if Rama is late Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 112, verses 9–29
  3. Enthrones the sandals at Nandigrama, holds the kingdom as a trust (nyasa), lives in bark and matted hair, and reports every matter to the sandals; called bhratri-vatsala Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 115, verses 12–27
  4. Hanuman finds him in bark and antelope skin; he faints with joy at the news Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda, Sarga 125, verses 29–40
  5. “As you gave this kingdom to me, so I give it back” Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda, Sarga 128, verses 1–5
  6. Called bhratri-vatsala, devoted to his brothers, again at Rama’s return Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda, Sarga 127, verse 56
