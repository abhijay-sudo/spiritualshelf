Gods & Puranas

Parvati

पार्वती

Uma · Shailaputri

Parvati, Uma, daughter of Himavan and Mena and younger sister of Ganga, is revered by every world as Shiva’s wife and Ganesha’s mother. Her tapas was fierce: fires around her in summer, water all winter, until she gave up even leaves and the gods named her Aparna. She formed Ganesha from her own body. When Ravana shook Kailasa she trembled and held fast to Shiva; when Vishnu was born as the dwarf Vamana, it was she who gave him his first alms.

Where this comes from

The notes this page about Parvati was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. Title: Valmiki names her Uma and calls her the mountain’s daughter (śailaduhitā at 35.20; śailaputrī at 37.6). Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda 35.16, 35.20–21
  2. Title: Valmiki names her Uma and calls her the mountain’s daughter (śailaduhitā at 35.20; śailaputrī at 37.6). Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda 37.6 (śailaputryā)
  3. Second daughter of Himavan and Mena, Ganga being the elder; performed fierce tapas; Himavan gave her, so endowed, to Rudra; revered by the worlds. Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda 35.14–16, 35.20–22 (ugraṁ tapaḥ; lokanamaskṛtām)
  4. Her penance: fires around her in summer, in water through winter; gave up even leaves, so the gods called her Aparna. Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda 22.40–42, 22.47–49 (tr. J. L. Shastri)
  5. Born to Mena, wife of Himavan. Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda 6
  6. Trembles and clings to Maheshvara when Ravana shakes the mountain (Kailasa in tradition and in this site’s story). Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda 16.26
  7. Mother of Ganesha, whom she forms from her own body and calls her son. Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Kumara Khanda 13.20–25
  8. With Shiva sets the contest and accepts Ganesha’s seven rounds of his parents. Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Kumara Khanda 19
  9. When Vishnu is born as Vamana, Uma, wife of Shiva, gives him his first alms. Bhagavata Purana 8.18.17 (bhikṣāṁ bhagavatī sākṣād umādād ambikā satī)
