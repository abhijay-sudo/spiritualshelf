Ramayana

Narada

नारद

Devarshi

Narada is the devarshi, the sage among the gods: given to austerity and Vedic study, best of the eloquent, knower of the three worlds; tradition pictures him with a veena, always singing of Narayana. As the Ramayana opens he answers Valmiki by naming Rama and telling his whole life in brief. Elsewhere on this site he teaches the boy Dhruva to seek Vishnu, shelters Prahlada’s mother, and foretells Satyavan’s death.
