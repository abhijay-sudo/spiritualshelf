Gods & Puranas

Nanda

नंद

Nanda Baba · Vrajapati

Nanda is the chief of the cowherds of Vraja, whom the Bhagavata calls great-hearted; Krishna, born to Devaki and Vasudeva, was laid in his house and raised as his son. Krishna persuades him to honour Govardhana instead of Indra, and when Indra’s storm breaks, Nanda and all Vraja shelter beneath the lifted hill. In these stories he embraces Krishna and blesses him when the hill is set down again.
