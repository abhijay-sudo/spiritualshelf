Mahabharata

Kunti

कुंती

Pritha · Mother of the Pandavas

Kunti, mother of the Pandavas, was born Pritha, sister of Krishna’s father. Durvasa, pleased with her service, gave her a mantra to call any god; by it she bore Karna to the Sun and set him adrift: “Even in far lands I shall know you by this armour.”

Read more

As Pandu’s queen she bore Yudhishthira, Bhima and Arjuna. Before the war she told Karna who he was, and he promised she would always have five sons. When it was over she asked Krishna for sorrows, again and again, for in them she saw him.

Where this comes from

What this page about Kunti was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. Sura, foremost of the Yadus and father of Vasudeva, gives his daughter Pritha to the childless Kuntibhoja, who makes her his daughter. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Adi Parva, Section LXVII
  2. Kunti tells how Durvasa, pleased with her service, gave her a mantra to summon any of the celestials. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Adi Parva, Section CXXII
  3. By the mantra she obtains Yudhishthira from Dharma, Bhima from the god of wind and Arjuna from Indra. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Adi Parva, Section CXXIII
  4. As a girl she invokes Surya with the mantra. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Vana Parva, Section CCCIV
  5. She carries the infant in a basket to the river Aswa at night and consigns him to its waters. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Vana Parva, Section CCCVI
  6. Krishna and Balarama touch the feet of Kunti, “their father’s sister”. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Adi Parva, Section 193
  7. Setting the infant adrift: “Even in foreign lands I shall be able to recognise thee by this mail of thine!” Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Vana Parva, Section CCCVI
  8. Kunti tells Karna he is her son; he pledges the safety of four of her sons and says her sons will never be fewer than five. Mahabharata (tr. K. M. Ganguli), Udyoga Parva, Sections CXLV–CXLVI
  9. After the war, as Krishna is leaving, Kunti prays: let those calamities come again and again, so that we may see you again and again (vipadaḥ santu tāḥ śaśvat … bhavato darśanaṁ yat syāt). Bhagavata Purana 1.8.25
About these sources
  • Section 193 is printed “CLXLIII” in the Ganguli edition.
