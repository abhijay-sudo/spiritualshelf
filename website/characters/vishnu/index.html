Gods & Puranas

Vishnu

विष्णु

Shri Hari · Narayana

Vishnu, called Hari and Narayana, is worshipped as the Lord who upholds the worlds; the Bhagavata says his descents protect them age after age. On this site he comes on Garuda to the boy Dhruva and grants him the pole star, and is born to Aditi as Vamana to win heaven back from Bali. When Mahishasura drives out the gods, the light of Vishnu’s anger is among the fires that form the Goddess.
