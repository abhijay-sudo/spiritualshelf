Ramayana

Kaikeyi

कैकेयी

Kekaya-nandini

Kaikeyi, princess of Kekaya and mother of Bharata, was a queen dear to Dasharatha; in a war of gods and demons she bore the wounded king from the field, saved his life, and was promised two boons. She rejoiced at the news of Rama’s crowning, saying she saw no difference between Rama and Bharata.

Read more

Then Manthara turned her joy to fear, and she claimed the boons: the throne for Bharata, fourteen years of exile for Rama.

Where this comes from

What this page about Kaikeyi was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. In the war of gods and demons against Shambara she carried the wounded, unconscious Dasharatha from the field and saved him; he granted two boons, to be asked when she wished Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 9, verses 11–17
  2. Dasharatha goes to her rooms to tell her of Rama’s consecration; she is called his beloved wife Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 10, verses 9–18
  3. Rejoices at the news, gives Manthara a jewel and says: I see no difference between Rama and Bharata Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 7, verses 31–36
  4. Praises Rama’s virtues to Manthara; he is as dear to her as Bharata Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 8, verses 13–19
  5. Won over by Manthara, “though right-minded by nature” Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 9, verses 1–3 and 36–37
  6. Claims the two boons: Bharata’s consecration and fourteen years in Dandaka for Rama Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 11, verses 18–29
  7. Tells Rama herself Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 18, verses 32–38
  8. Rama to Bharata: she acted out of love for you; protect mother Kaikeyi, do not be angry with her Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sarga 112, verses 19 and 27–28
  9. “Kekaya-nandini”, daughter of Kekaya, as her traditional epithet Ramcharitmanas of Tulsidas, Ayodhya Kand, doha 91 and the chaupai following doha 158, where she is called “kaikayanandini”
