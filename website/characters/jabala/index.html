Upanishads

Jabala

जबाला

Mother of Satyakama

Jabala is the mother of Satyakama in the Chandogya Upanishad. When her son asked of what family he was, she said she did not know: in her youth she had moved about a great deal as a serving woman, and he was born to her then.

Read more

She gave him her name and his own, and told him to say that he was Satyakama Jabala. He repeated her answer to his teacher exactly, and the teacher said he had not swerved from the truth. The Upanishad itself calls her son Satyakama Jabala, in its first line about him and again later when he is a teacher.

Where this comes from

What this page about Jabala was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. Name: jabālā, 4.4.1-2 (the mother is Jabālā; the son’s name from hers is Jābāla). Sources of the story “Satyakama Jabala”
  2. ‘I do not know’; in her youth moving about much as a serving woman (bahv ahaṃ carantī paricāriṇī yauvane tvām alabhe); ‘I am Jabala, you are Satyakama; say you are Satyakama Jabala’: 4.4.2. Sources of the story “Satyakama Jabala”
  3. The son repeats it exactly and the teacher says he has not swerved from the truth: 4.4.4-5. Sources of the story “Satyakama Jabala”
  4. The Upanishad itself calls him Satyakama Jabala: satyakāmo ha jābālo at 4.4.1 (before the mother speaks) and satyakāme jābāle at 4.10.1. Sources of the story “Satyakama Jabala”
