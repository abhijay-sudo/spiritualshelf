Ramayana

Vishvamitra

विश्वामित्र

Brahmarshi

Vishvamitra, born a king, the son of Gadhi, is revered as the one who became a brahmarshi by tapas alone. When Vasishtha’s staff quelled all his weapons, he sighed, shame on a kshatriya’s might, and turned to austerity until Brahma himself named him brahmarshi. Tradition holds him the seer of the Gayatri mantra. He took the young Rama from Ayodhya, gave him the divine weapons and the art of recalling them, and led him to Shiva’s bow.

Where this comes from

The notes this page about Vishvamitra was checked against: each point, and where it is found.

  1. A king for many years, son of Gadhi in the line of Kusha Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 51, verses 16–20
  2. Vasishtha’s staff quells all his weapons; “the strength of a kshatriya is no strength”; he resolves on austerity Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 56, verses 1–24
  3. Begins his austerities; Brahma first grants him the rank of royal sage Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 57, verses 1–9
  4. Brahma and the gods greet him as brahmarshi, and Vasishtha confirms it Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 65, verses 19–27
  5. Asks Dasharatha for Rama to guard his rite Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 19, verses 4–9
  6. Gives Rama the divine weapons Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 27, verses 1–28
  7. Teaches Rama the withdrawal of the weapons Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 28, verses 1–15
  8. “Let the bow be shown to Rama”; tells Rama to look at it Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 67, verses 1 and 12
  9. Seer of the Gayatri mantra Tradition, not the cited text: the Gayatri (Rigveda 3.62.10) stands in the third mandala, whose hymns tradition assigns to Vishvamitra; not stated in the Ramayana, and worded in the text as tradition.
  10. After Vasishtha’s staff quells his weapons: “Fie on a kshatriya’s might; the might of brahma-tejas is might indeed” Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda, Sarga 56, verse 23 (dhig balam kshatriya-balam brahma-tejo-balam balam)
