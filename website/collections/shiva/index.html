Ganga Avataran: The river in Shiva’s hair

A king stands a year on one toe. Heaven’s river leaps to sweep away the god who catches her, and is lost for years in his locks, until one stream turns to follow a chariot down to the ashes of the dead.

Brahma and Vishnu go to war over who is lord. A pillar of fire rises between them. One seeks its foot, one its top. One comes back with a lie.

Three cities of gold, silver and iron can fall only to a single arrow, in the one hour they meet. Shiva takes aim from a chariot made of the worlds.
