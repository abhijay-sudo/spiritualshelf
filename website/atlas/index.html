Scroll worlds

  • A scroll worldHanuman’s LeapFrom Mahendra mountain, across a hundred yojanas of ocean, to the shore of Lanka. Scroll, and travel it with him.
  • A scroll worldThe Descent of GangaThe river of heaven falls, is lost for years in Shiva’s hair, and runs free to the sea. Scroll, and the water comes down beside the words.
  • A scroll worldThe Fourteen LokasThe Vishnu Purana counts seven spheres from earth up to Satya-loka and seven regions of Patala below. Scroll down through them, in its own names and words.

How this map was made

Coastline and rivers: Natural Earth (public domain), simplified here. Relief: shaded here from the Terrain Tiles open dataset on AWS, which contains SRTM and GMTED2010 content courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey and ETOPO1 content courtesy of the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration. Pin positions: Wikidata coordinates (CC0) of the modern place each source names. Natural Earth calls the river at Ayodhya the Ghaghara; the Ayodhya district website calls it the Saryu, the Sarayu of the story.
