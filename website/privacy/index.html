Privacy & your reading

Spiritual Shelf has no accounts and no ads, and no analytics runs unless you allow it. What the site remembers about your reading stays in this browser, on this device, is never sent to us, and you can erase it at any time. Like any website, our hosting service receives ordinary connection details when pages load; these are listed under What leaves your device.

What this browser keeps

Each of these is saved in your browser’s storage only when you use the feature it belongs to. It stays until you remove it or erase everything (below). It is not encrypted and not locked: anyone who uses this browser could see it.

  • Saved stories. The stories you mark with Save, listed on Your reading.
  • Your reflections. What you write under “What stays with you?” at the end of a story, with the story and the date. Up to 200 are kept. You can remove any one of them on Your reading.
  • Days with a story. The dates on which you read at least halfway through a story or kept a reflection (the last 120), used only to draw the day marks on Your reading and Today. Days you did not read are never recorded.
  • Where you stopped. The place you reached in each story you have opened (the scene and when), so Continue reading, the marks on the library cards and the “Pick up at scene…” line on a story can work. If you open the Quiet moment from a story, also that story’s title and question, your place on the page and the time, so you can return to it within a day. “Forget my place” on Your reading clears all of this.
  • Lines you keep. The single line from the end of a story, with its source and the story’s title, only if you press “Keep this”. Up to 80 are kept. You can remove any one of them on Your reading.
  • Today’s choices. Your answer to “What do you need today?” and an intention you keep on Today, each with the day’s date, and whether you chose to read in the morning or the evening.
  • Reading settings. Text size, appearance, motion, your reading edition (English, हिन्दी or side by side) and how pages turn.
  • Sound. Whether you turned sound on.
  • Hindi suggestion. If your browser asks for Hindi, the English home page offers the Hindi edition; that you closed or followed that note, so it is not shown again.
  • Ramcharitmanas transliteration. Whether you chose to show or hide the transliteration under each line.
  • The opening. A note that you have already seen the welcome animation and the opening entrances, so they do not play every time.
  • Your privacy choice. Whether you allowed or declined analytics, and the date you chose, so the question is not asked again for 12 months.

While a tab is open the browser also holds short notes about which painting to carry from one page to the next and whether the home page’s opening titles have already played. They disappear when the tab closes.

Copies for reading offline

After your first visit a service worker (a small script your browser runs for this site) keeps copies of the pages you open, up to 160 paintings and the fonts, so stories still open on a weak or missing connection. These are copies of the public site, not notes about you, though they do show which pages were opened in this browser. Film clips are never stored.

What leaves your device

  • Our hosting service (Vercel) sends you the pages, paintings, typefaces, film and sound. Like any website’s host, it receives ordinary connection details: your IP address, the address asked for and your browser type.
  • Google Analytics, only if you press Allow, receives what is described under Analytics, only with your permission (below).
  • Listen reads aloud with a voice built into your device or browser. Some browsers use an online voice service for some voices, which would send the text being read to that service. That choice belongs to your browser, not to Spiritual Shelf.
  • Links you follow, such as source links and the correction form on GitHub, open other websites with their own privacy practices. Corrections sent through GitHub are public.

Your saved stories, reflections and reading dates are never sent anywhere. Copying a line and making a verse card happen on your device.

Analytics, only with your permission

On your first visit a small bar asks whether we may use Google Analytics to count visits. Until you press Allow, nothing from Google Analytics is loaded: no script, no cookie and no request to Google. If you press Decline, nothing loads, and you are not asked again for 12 months. Your answer is kept only in this browser.

If you press Allow, Google Analytics 4 is loaded from googletagmanager.com. It receives the address of each page you open (without anything after a ? or #) and its title, the page you came from (shortened the same way), your browser, device type, screen size and language, and an approximate location that Google works out from your IP address. It also receives five kinds of events: that you began or finished a story (the story’s short name and the language), opened the Hanuman Chalisa or the Shiva Tandava Stotram, opened a character’s page, or switched between English and Hindi. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_P7YG57M2CP holding a random identifier, so that return visits from this browser can be counted; they last up to two years after your last visit unless you withdraw. When you switch language, the new language is noted in this tab until the next page sends it.

Google Analytics also measures a few things by itself, as part of its standard measurement for this site: that you scrolled to near the end of a page; that you began filling in a form on the page (such as the search box, a reflection or an intention), with the page’s address, the number of fields and the name of the first field, never what you type; that you followed a link to another website or to a file there, with the link’s address and its words; and how long the page was in view.

Never sent: your reflections, saved stories, reading days, intentions, searches or anything else you type. Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off, advertising consent stays denied, and we do not use this data for advertising.

Google provides Google Analytics to us as a processor, under Google’s terms for the service, and the data may be processed on Google’s servers outside India. Google Analytics keeps event-level data for 2 months, its default setting; counts in its reports (such as how often a story was opened) do not identify you and are kept longer.

You can change your answer at any time with Privacy choices at the foot of every page, or with the button below. If you withdraw, Analytics stops sending from that moment and its _ga cookies are removed from this site. What was already sent cannot be removed from your browser; Google deletes event-level data at the end of the retention period.

You have not chosen yet, so analytics is off.

Erase everything

This removes everything listed on this page from this browser: saved stories, reflections, reading days, your place, settings, your privacy choice, the offline copies and any Google Analytics cookies. It cannot be undone. You can also clear the site data for this site in your browser’s settings.

Erase everything? Your saved stories, reflections, lines you kept, reading days, the places you stopped, your settings and the offline copies will be removed from this browser. This cannot be undone.
